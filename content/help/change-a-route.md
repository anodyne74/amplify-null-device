To add or remove Properties on a Route you've already asked for, email [{{requestsEmail}}](mailto:{{requestsEmail}}). That email is a Route Amendment. You can send as many as you need, and we keep every one, oldest first.

## What to include

- **Which Route**: its route code (shown at the top of the Route, for example Route W41-26-003), or its Placement Date.
- **What to change**: the Properties to add or take off.
- **A revised Schedule**, if you have one. A sentence in the email is fine too.

## Change a date

To move a Route's Placement Date or Pickup Date, email [{{requestsEmail}}](mailto:{{requestsEmail}}) with the route code and the date you need. We'll change it if we can, and the Route shows the new date.

## When to send it

Send changes as early as you can. Once signs are going up, some changes may not be possible on that day.

## Seeing your changes

Each Route Amendment appears in the Route's **Requests** section once we've recorded it, under your original Route Request. We then update the Route's Stops to match.

## Instructions for one Route

For something that isn't a change of Properties, such as a gate code or a street to avoid, add a special instruction on the Route itself. See [Follow a Route](/customer/help/follow-a-route).
