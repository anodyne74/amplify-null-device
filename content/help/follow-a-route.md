**Routes** lists every Route we've set up for you. Search by route code, then open a Route to see its detail.

## On a Route

- **Route status** shows where the Route is up to: signs placed first, then picked up.
- **Placement date** and **Pickup date** are the days we plan to put your signs up and take them down. To change a date, see [Change a Route](/customer/help/change-a-route).
- **Stops** is the number of Stops on the Route, one for each Property we visit.
- **Signs out** is the Signs Placed: every sign put out on the Route.
- The progress bar counts Stops placed, then Stops picked up.
- **Signs missing** appears once collection has started, if any signs couldn't be found.
- **Duration** shows the Billed Time once the Route is complete.

The map and the list of Stops show each Property. Tap a Stop to find it on the map.

## Each Stop

Each Stop shows how it's going:

- **Awaiting placement**: its signs aren't up yet.
- **Placed**: its signs are up.
- **Awaiting pickup**: its signs are up, waiting to be collected.
- **Picked up**: its signs have been collected.
- **Not yet collected**: we couldn't take its signs down, for example because a gate was locked. They're still at the Property, and we'll collect them.
- **Removed on the day**: the Stop came off the Route on the day, so its signs didn't go up.

A Stop with Missing Signs shows how many couldn't be found. Missing Signs never count as Signs Collected.

## Special instructions

Add anything we should know for this Route only, such as access details, extra signs or a street to avoid. You can add instructions until sign placement begins. For instructions that apply to every Route, see [Route Defaults](/customer/help/route-defaults).

## When the Route is complete

We ask how it went. See [Give feedback on a Route](/customer/help/route-feedback).
