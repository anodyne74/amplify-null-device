We put your signs up at the Properties you choose, then take them down again. This portal shows each Route as it happens.

## How it works

1. **You ask for a Route.** Email a Route Request to [{{requestsEmail}}](mailto:{{requestsEmail}}) with your Schedule attached and the dates you need. See [Request a Route](/customer/help/request-a-route).
2. **We set it up.** The Route appears under **Routes** with its Placement Date, its Pickup Date and a Stop for each Property.
3. **We put your signs up** on the Placement Date. You can watch each Stop being placed. See [Follow a Route](/customer/help/follow-a-route).
4. **We collect them** on the Pickup Date.
5. **You tell us how it went.** Once the Route is complete, we ask for your Route Feedback. See [Give feedback on a Route](/customer/help/route-feedback).
6. **We invoice you** for the Billed Time. See [Invoices and Billed Time](/customer/help/invoices-and-billed-time).

Need to change something after you've asked? See [Change a Route](/customer/help/change-a-route).

## Your dashboard

The dashboard shows this week at a glance. Account Owners see what's been invoiced and what's outstanding, and what each agent has cost. Other users see the current Route, Stops this week, signs in the field and recent Routes.

## Words we use

The portal uses a few words in a particular way, such as Placement Date and Missing Signs. The [Glossary](/customer/help/glossary) explains each one.
