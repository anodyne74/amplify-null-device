---
title: Glossary
summary: The words this portal uses, and what they mean.
order: 11
---

**Route**: one sign run for you: we put your signs up at a set of Properties, then take them down.

**Stop**: one visit to a Property on a Route, where we put signs up and later take them down.

**Property**: a real address. Many Stops, on many Routes, can be at the same Property.

**Placement Date**: the day we plan to put a Route's signs up.

**Pickup Date**: the day we plan to take a Route's signs down. It's set from what you asked for, or from your sign collection day.

**Route Request**: your email asking us to set up one Route, usually with its Schedule attached. See [Request a Route](/customer/help/request-a-route).

**Route Amendment**: a later email adding or removing Properties on a Route you've already asked for. See [Change a Route](/customer/help/change-a-route).

**Schedule**: the file you send listing the Properties for a Route. We keep it exactly as you sent it.

**Signs Placed**: every sign put out on a Route, shown as **Signs out**.

**Signs Collected**: the signs we brought back, not counting Missing Signs or signs not yet collected.

**Missing Signs**: signs we couldn't find when we went to collect them.

**Couldn't Collect**: a Stop whose signs we couldn't take down, for example because a gate was locked. It shows as **not yet collected**. The signs are still at the Property, and we'll collect them.

**Removed Stop**: a Stop that came off the Route on the day, so its signs didn't go up. It shows as **removed on the day**.

**Billed Time**: what you're charged for a Route. See [Invoices and Billed Time](/customer/help/invoices-and-billed-time).

**Route Feedback**: your answer to "How did this route go?" once a Route is complete. See [Give feedback on a Route](/customer/help/route-feedback).

**Account Owner**: a user who can see invoices and billing, and manage Route Defaults and Property History Reports.

**Route Defaults**: what every new Route we set up for you starts from. See [Route Defaults](/customer/help/route-defaults).
