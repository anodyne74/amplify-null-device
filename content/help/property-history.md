Property History lists your Visits: each time one of your Routes had a Stop at a Property.

## Search

1. Start typing a suburb, street or address, then pick one from the suggestions.
2. Narrow it down by date, agent or auction if you like.

Each Visit links to its Route. Upcoming Routes are listed separately as scheduled.

## Property History Reports

Account Owners can save a search as a Property History Report: search, then select the PDF button above the results. The report is a PDF snapshot of that search on the day you made it.

The **Reports** tab lists your reports. Each one is kept for 30 days, and you can remove one sooner.
