To ask for a Route, send an email to [{{requestsEmail}}](mailto:{{requestsEmail}}). That email is your Route Request. Send one email for each Route.

## What to include

- **Your Schedule**: the file listing the Properties, usually a spreadsheet saved as a PDF. Attach it to the email.
- **The Placement Date**: the day you want your signs to go up.
- **The Pickup Date**: the day you want them taken down. If you leave it out, we'll use your sign collection day from Route Defaults, or the day after the Placement Date.
- Anything we should know, such as how many signs go at each Property.

## Before you choose dates

The **Calendar** shows the days we can't put signs up or take them down, and the days your agency is closed. Pick a Placement Date and a Pickup Date that aren't marked.

Account Owners can mark a day closed on the Calendar, with a reason if you like, so we know not to plan anything for you that day. Select the day, then **Mark closed**.

## What happens next

We set up the Route from your Schedule. It appears under **Routes**, and its **Requests** section keeps your email as we received it: who sent it, when, and the attached Schedule.

We keep your Schedule exactly as you sent it. If something needs to change, send a Route Amendment instead of editing the Schedule. See [Change a Route](/customer/help/change-a-route).
