**Settings** changes how the portal looks for you. It doesn't affect anyone else on your account.

- **Name**: how your name appears in the portal.
- **Default Theme**: light or dark.
- **Map Theme**: how maps look, for example on a Route.

Save your changes and they apply straight away.
