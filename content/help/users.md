**Users** lists everyone who can sign in to your portal.

## Two kinds of user

- **Account Owner**: sees everything, including invoices and billing, and manages Route Defaults and Property History Reports.
- **Read only**: sees Routes and Stops only.

:::if account-owner-invite
## Add a user

1. Enter their email and, if you like, a display name.
2. Select **Send invite**.

They'll get an email with a link to set their own password and sign in. New users are read only. To make someone an Account Owner, email [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}).
:::else
## Add a user

Email [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}) with their name and email address, and say whether they should be an Account Owner.
:::end

## Remove a user

Email [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}) and we'll remove their access.
